    Is it free to attend?

    Yes. Attendee registration is free on Luma. Click any "Register free" button and you'll go straight to the event page.

    How do I book an exhibitor table?

    Fill in the short form on this page, choose your category and package, then pay by card or bank transfer. Our team will confirm your table and send your invoice.

    What's the difference between Early Bird and Standard?

    Only the price. Early Bird is $99 for the first 5 exhibitors. After that, Standard is $199 with the same benefits.

    Why only one business per category?

    We're limiting the event to 10 complementary businesses, so each exhibitor has their category to themselves. Once a category is booked, it no longer shows in the form.

    Can I present on stage?

    Premium Partners get a 3 to 5 minute presentation and a mention by the MC. Early Bird and Standard exhibitors get a 1-minute introduction during networking.

# AI E-Commerce Catalyst

Landing page prototype for **AI E-Commerce Catalyst @ Western Sydney Startup Hub** (Insights • Expo • Business Networking), organised by Cloudnetic Events.

Live preview: https://developerjillur.github.io/ai-ecommerce-catalyst/

## What's on the page

- Event details with a live countdown: Friday 27 November 2026, 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM, North Parramatta NSW
- Free attendee registration through Luma
- A board of the 10 exhibitor tables, one business per category
- Early Bird ($99, first 5), Standard ($199) and Premium Partner ($249, only 2) packages
- A three-step exhibitor booking form with card or bank transfer
- An organiser demo panel to change the event details and try the package limits

## Notes

This is a design prototype. Nothing on the page sends data or takes payment. Contact details, bank details, social links and the speaker photo are placeholders until they are confirmed. Photos are placeholders too.

Event settings live in one object at the top of the script in `index.html`, so a new date or time updates the whole page.
